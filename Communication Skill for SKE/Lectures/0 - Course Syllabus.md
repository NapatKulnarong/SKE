## Grading

| Component | Weight | Breakdown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Participation** | 20% | 10% time + 10% Q&A |
| **Assignment** | 10% | 5% time + 5% correctness |
| **Presentation** | 40% | 10% organization + 10% delivery + 10% content relevance + 10% audience engagement |
| **Examination** | 30% | 15% your presentation + 15% classmate presentation |

Time is scored because finishing on time is part of the skill. Q&A and classmate presentations score **listening**, not only speaking.
