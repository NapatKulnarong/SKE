- B. Always **overrides** a higher law during an emergency

- B. The Constitution
- C. A Code of Law (Civil/Commerce, Penal, Land)
- D. A Ministerial Regulation

- A. Sits above the Constitution
- B. Extends the Constitution but **cannot override** it
- C. Is issued by a ministry for general administration

- B. Governs royal succession, **not** laws for the general public

- A. Consistent contracts, commerce, and inheritance

- A. Plaintiff vs. defendant
- B. Controller vs. processor

- A. Local/subordinate laws only

- C. Commerce (civil) law

- A. PDPA Section 73 only
- B. Article 12 of the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (privacy, family, home, correspondence)

- A. Only storing data in the EU

- A. Is explicitly required by APPI, not GDPR

- A. Article 15
- B. Article 17
- C. Article 22
- D. Article 82

**17.** PDPA **Section 31** is the right to:

- A. Access your own personal data and know how it was collected
- B. Receive your data in a **portable, machine-readable** format

**18.** GDPR applies to **EU data subjects, anywhere in the world**. PDPA applies to:

- A. EU data subjects only
- B. Anyone **physically in Thailand**

- A. 16
- B. 13
- C. 10
- D. 18

**20.** Compared with GDPR, APPI:

- A. Requires a DPO and fully defines pseudonymized data

**21.** **Data portability** is the right to get a **copy** of your personal data in a format you can **reuse or transfer** to another service, instead of it being:

- A. Hashed with a checksum
- B. Locked into one company’s system
- C. Covered by a patent

**22.** Unlike privacy laws, computer crime laws **usually do not apply across borders**, so this lecture covers:

- A. Only GDPR
- B. Only **Thai** computer crime law
- C. Only APPI
- D. Only US CFAA

**23.** Computer Crimes Act **Section 5** criminalizes:

- A. Spreading false information
- B. Unauthorized **access to a system**
- C. Distributing malware (that is Section 12)

**24.** **Section 14** (spreading false information) is flagged because it is:

- A. Never used in practice

**26.** The **Cybersecurity Act (2562/2019)** establishes the **NCSC** and defines critical information infrastructure as including national security, government services, banking, telecom, transportation/logistics, energy/utilities, health, and:

- A. Only GitHub repositories
- B. Others as **designated by the NCSC**
- C. Only EU data subjects

**27.** **Copyright** in creative work is **automatic** (no registration). Pushing code to GitHub:
